# Voltage (Water Analogy Mapping)

## Evolution Log
- 2026-04-24: 来自 [[Ohms_Law_Water_Analogy]] 的认知：In the Ohm's Law water analogy, voltage (V) is mapped to water pressure, defined as the force that pushes flow through the system, with higher pressure corresponding to higher voltage.
