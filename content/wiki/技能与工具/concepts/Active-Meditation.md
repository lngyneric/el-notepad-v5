# Active Meditation

## Evolution Log
- 2026-04-24: 来自 [[session-02-meditation]] 的认知：A core meditation practice format where practitioners proactively maintain present-moment awareness, letting go of daydreams and returning focus to a chosen anchor (e.g., breath) as soon as attention drift is detected.
