# Flow Prerequisites

## Evolution Log
- 2026-04-24: 来自 [[session-05-flow]] 的认知：The three core conditions required to achieve flow: the activity must be meaningful, the activity must be appropriately challenging, and the individual must work undisturbed, with the undisturbed work condition identified as the most difficult to implement in typical modern workplaces.
